About Me

Hi, I am Antonio Jr. S. Del Rosario, a third-year Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) student at the University of Mindanao, Davao City, Davao Del Sur.

I am driven by a passion for software development, web technologies, digital innovation, and technology-driven solutions.

I believe that becoming a competent IT professional requires continuous learning, practical experience, ethical decision-making, and the willingness to adapt to an ever-changing industry. My goals go beyond technical knowledge — I aim to strengthen my problem-solving abilities, communication skills, professional discipline, and understanding of how technology creates value for people and businesses.

Through this educational tour, I hope to gain a clearer understanding of the professional environment I aspire to join and the responsibilities that come with becoming an IT practitioner.
